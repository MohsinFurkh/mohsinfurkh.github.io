Research Profile

I develop deep learning methods for ultrasound image analysis that treat ambiguity — at lesion boundaries and across datasets — as a signal rather than noise, so that models stay reliable on the small, heterogeneous datasets typical of point-of-care imaging. My doctoral work introduced a fuzzy rough set loss (Computerized Medical Imaging and Graphics, 2026) and an adaptive ensemble loss (Medical & Biological Engineering & Computing, 2025) for segmentation, and efficient, interpretable classifiers for breast ultrasound (Neural Processing Letters, 2023; Image and Vision Computing, 2024; Biomedical Signal Processing and Control, 2026).

At UPES I extended this work to breast, thyroid and fetal ultrasound as first and corresponding author of MSCT-Trans (Ultrasound in Medicine & Biology, 2026), written without my doctoral advisor. My current agenda extends uncertainty-aware learning to maternal–fetal Doppler ultrasound, the subject of an ICMR ANVESHAN proposal I submitted as Principal Investigator (under review).

Record: 9 publications since 2022 (7 as first author; 4 in SJR Q1 journals); 115 citations and h-index 4 (Google Scholar, September 2026); code released publicly for 6 papers; reviewer for 8 journals, including IEEE TMI and IEEE JBHI.
